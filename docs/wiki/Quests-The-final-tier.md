## Tier 49

_This is the last tier. When you finish it, it starts over, so the same kinds of task keep coming._

Each time you ask for a task you are offered up to 3 to pick from.

**Rewards you must earn to finish this tier (in this order):**

1. Cashbot Upper Left Leg Part
2. Cashbot Lower Left Leg Part
3. Cashbot Left Foot Part
4. Cashbot Upper Right Leg Part
5. Cashbot Lower Right Leg Part
6. Cashbot Right Foot Part
7. Cashbot Upper Torso Part
8. Cashbot Pelvis Part
9. Cashbot Upper Left Arm Part
10. Cashbot Lower Left Arm Part
11. Cashbot Upper Right Arm Part
12. Cashbot Lower Right Arm Part

**Just for fun rewards** (extra tasks, not needed to move on): Clothing Ticket x2, 500 jellybeans, 600 jellybeans, 700 jellybeans, 800 jellybeans, 900 jellybeans, 1000 jellybeans, 1100 jellybeans, 1200 jellybeans, 1300 jellybeans, Big head, Small head, Big legs, Small legs, Big toon, Small toon, Flat portrait, Flat profile, Transparent, No color, Invisible toon
