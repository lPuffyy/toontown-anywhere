## Tier 7

Each time you ask for a task you are offered up to 3 to pick from.

**Rewards you must earn to finish this tier (in this order):**

1. 1 point Laff boost
2. Small Bag (35)
3. 2 point Laff boost
4. 3 point Laff boost
5. 1 point Laff boost
6. 2 point Laff boost
7. Carry 3 ToonTasks
8. Carry 80 jellybeans
9. Teleport access to Garden Grove

**Just for fun rewards** (extra tasks, not needed to move on): Clothing Ticket, 60 jellybeans x2, 100 jellybeans x2, Big head, Small head, Big legs, Small legs, Big toon, Small toon
