Toontown on mobile

Toontown: Anywhere is the classic Toontown Online game, playable on your phone. It runs the whole game in the browser, with touch controls made for phones and tablets. You can also play with a keyboard or a game controller. Make a toon, train your gags, fight Cogs with friends, go fishing, and decorate your estate.

Install it like an app

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open toontown.puffsco.com/play in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (tap … first if you don't see it).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open TT: Anywhere from your Home Screen.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open toontown.puffsco.com/play in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Open TT: Anywhere from your Home screen.

Good to know

  • The first start downloads the game files (about 390 MB), so use Wi-Fi. They stay on your device after that.
  • Pick a name and a password, then tap Play. Use the same ones next time.
  • Stuck on a task? The quest guide lists every Toontask and where to go.

Questions

Can I play Toontown on my phone?

Yes. Toontown: Anywhere runs in your web browser on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, with touch controls built for small screens. Open toontown.puffsco.com/play, make a toon and play.

Is there a Toontown mobile app?

You play Toontown on mobile right in your browser, with nothing to download from an app store. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens full screen like an app.

Do I need to download an app?

No app store download is needed. The game runs in Safari or Chrome, and you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.

Is it free?

Yes. Toontown: Anywhere is free to play, with no purchases.

Can I play with friends on a computer?

Yes. Phones, tablets and computers all play on the same server, so you can team up with friends on any device.
